Pearl Circle

Not a points program. A relationship.

We don't believe in making you collect points to earn our attention. From your first stay, you're known. Pearl Circle simply means the longer you stay with us, the more we can do for you.

Membership Tiers

Oyster

Entry — automatic on first booking

  • Complimentary WiFi
  • Welcome amenity
  • Pearl Journal delivered to room
  • Access to member rates
  • 10% off spa treatments

Nacre

After 5 nights or 2 stays

Includes all Oyster benefits PLUS:

  • Room upgrade (subject to availability)
  • Late checkout until 2pm
  • Complimentary breakfast for two
  • Early access to experience bookings
  • Birthday amenity

Lustré

After 15 nights or 6 stays

Includes all Nacre benefits PLUS:

  • Guaranteed upgrade (including suites)
  • 4pm late checkout
  • Complimentary airport transfers
  • Dedicated Pearl Host at each property
  • Access to members-only experiences
  • Annual complimentary night

Black Pearl

By invitation only

Includes all Lustré benefits PLUS:

  • Guaranteed best available suite
  • No blackout dates
  • Private check-in
  • Personal travel curator (24/7)
  • Chef's table priority at all restaurants
  • Complimentary minibar
  • Invitation to annual Pearl Gala
