Our Story

From one hotel in Dubai to ten on four continents. Always family-owned. Always independent.

A Timeline of Excellence

2006

The Beginning

Arjun Mehta opens Pearl Dubai, a 220-room oceanfront hotel built on the principle that luxury should feel like generosity, not performance. First-year occupancy: 94%.

2009

First Expansion

Pearl Marrakech opens — a restored 19th-century riad. The first property outside the UAE. Forbes calls it "the most thoughtful hotel in North Africa."

2011

European Debut

Pearl Santorini opens in Oia. Only 32 suites. Wait list within 6 months. Condé Nast Traveler names it Best New Hotel in Europe.

2013

Kyoto Opens

Pearl Kyoto opens — the first international luxury ryokan. Chef Takeda's Hikari restaurant earns 2 Michelin stars within 18 months.

2015

Island Paradise

Pearl Maldives opens. First luxury resort in the Baa Atoll to achieve carbon-neutral certification. Underwater restaurant Below becomes the most-requested reservation in the Maldives.

2016

Pearl Circle Launches

Pearl Circle loyalty program launches. 10,000 members in the first quarter. Black Pearl tier introduced — by invitation only.

2017

Patagonia

Pearl Patagonia opens at the base of Torres del Paine. 18 rooms. No TV. First Pearl property powered entirely by renewable energy.

2019

New Leadership

Pearl Amalfi and Pearl Cape Town open in the same month. Arjun Mehta steps from CEO to Chairman. Sofia Lindström, previously MD of Pearl Europe, becomes CEO.

2022

Ten Properties

Pearl Rajasthan opens — the palace restoration took 4 years. Pearl Tulum opens 3 months later. Portfolio reaches 10 properties.

2024

Global Recognition

Pearl Group named Leading Independent Hotel Group by World Travel Awards for the third consecutive year. Pearl Circle reaches 85,000 members globally.

Leadership

Arjun Mehta

Founder & Chairman

"I grew up in hotels. My father managed a 40-room guesthouse in Jaipur. I learned that hospitality isn't about thread count — it's about making someone feel that their comfort is your purpose. Everything Pearl has become started with that lesson."

Sofia Lindström

Chief Executive Officer

"Arjun built the soul of Pearl. My job is to scale it without losing it. Every time we open a new property, I ask one question: would Arjun's father recognize this as hospitality? If the answer is no, we start over."
